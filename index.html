Native = Meta lead-ad forms. Website = clicked a Meta ad and filled a landing-page form (UTMs/fbclid, or tagged meta-website in GHL). Calls = Meta call-tracking numbers (shows 0 until those are set up). Cost per lead uses total Meta spend for the whole ad account, so it's shown for All markets only. Consults / Quotes / Sold / Installed = how far each lead has got, based on its current pipeline stage (a Sold lead also counts as a Consult and Quote). Sales follow-up counts calls, texts and emails sent by a team member (automated workflow messages excluded). Revenue counts won deals for leads in the range; recent leads may not have closed yet. Data refreshes every 12 hours.
